CASE STUDY / Tide

Ti de.

A travel website designed for people who want to discover and experience the oceans of Africa. The experience combines destination discovery, visual storytelling and simple travel planning into one warm and inviting website.

WEB DESIGN UX/UI DESIGN TRAVEL AFRICA
01 CONTEXT

Discovering Africa through the ocean.

Tide is a travel website created to help visitors discover beautiful ocean destinations across Africa. The design focuses on making exploration feel exciting, visual and easy to navigate.

02 WEBSITE DESIGN

The full experience.

Designed as a long-form travel experience, allowing visitors to move naturally from inspiration to destination discovery.

03 DESIGN DIRECTION

Warm, playful and made for exploration.

The visual direction uses warm colours and soft shapes to create a feeling of sunshine, beaches and travel.

TYPOGRAPHY Baloo 2 + Inter

Baloo 2 gives the design a friendly, playful feel, while Inter keeps the supporting text clean, simple and easy to read.

COLOUR PALETTE

A soft neutral palette with warm coral and deep teal creates a balance between calm, playful and modern. The neutral tones keep the interface clean, while coral adds energy and teal creates contrast.

04 USER EXPERIENCE

From inspiration to exploration.

01 Discover

Introduce visitors to beautiful African ocean destinations.

02 Explore

Help users browse destinations and understand what makes each location special.

03 Plan

Give visitors useful information as they move closer to planning their trip.

07 OUTCOME

Turning destination discovery into an experience.

01

A warmer visual identity that reflects the feeling of travel and the ocean.

02

A clear structure that allows visitors to explore destinations naturally.

03

Large imagery gives the destinations the attention they deserve.

04

A responsive layout keeps the experience clean across different screen sizes.
